About IECS / IECS

Engineering grounded
in real work.

Based in Bhosari, Pune, Industrial Engineering Control Services brings manufacturing and maintenance experience to industrial automation, machine building and plant engineering.

Engineering concept · Illustrative image

Our perspective

The process comes first.
The solution follows.

IECS was founded by an electrical engineer with 10+ years of core experience in manufacturing, maintenance and engineering solutions. That experience informs our approach to production requirements.

Our services span industrial automation, special-purpose and press machines, mechanical tooling and fixtures, testing setups, control panels and industrial electrical work. We also develop control software, traceability solutions and hydraulic and pneumatic system controls.

From process development and prototyping to complete machine building, fabrication, installation and commissioning, IECS supports the equipment lifecycle. Plant services include annual maintenance, machine relocation, pneumatic pipeline work, and lifting hoist and jib crane installation.
